I'm a Dual Degree PhD student at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/) (Software Engineering) and [University of Porto](https://sigarra.up.pt/feup/en/web_page.inicial) (Computer Science). My advisors are [Dr Claire Le Goues](https://clairelegoues.com/), [Dr Rui Abreu](https://ruimaranhao.com/) and [Dr Jose Campos](https://jose.github.io/).
  
My primary interests lie in the application of <b>Large Language Models</b> to ensure <b>Code Quality and Safety</b>. I am particularly interested in vulnerability detection and, more recently, automated program repair and patch generation.
