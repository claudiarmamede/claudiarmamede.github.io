Cláudia Mamede

👋 Nice to meet you!

My name is Claudia. I’m a PhD student at Carnegie Mellon University and University of Porto advised by Dr Claire Le Goues, Dr Rui Abreu and Dr Jose Campos.

My primary interests lie in the application of Large Language Models to ensure Code Quality and Safety. I am particularly interested in vulnerability detection and, more recently, automated program repair and patch generation.

If you have any questions or just want to chat – hit me up! I’m always happy to discuss ✨ new ideas ✨
